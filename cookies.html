Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

CS Master Pro uses browser storage technologies (localStorage and IndexedDB) rather than traditional cookies. These technologies serve similar purposes but store data locally on your device.

2. How We Use Browser Storage

We use browser storage for the following purposes:

Essential Storage (Required)

These are necessary for the application to function properly:

Storage Key Purpose Duration
csmaster_session Keeps you logged in Until logout
csmaster_users Stores encrypted account data Persistent
csmaster_user Your current progress and settings Persistent

Functionality Storage

These enhance your experience:

Storage Key Purpose Duration
csmaster_daily_goals Tracks your daily learning goals 24 hours
csmaster_streak Maintains your study streak Persistent
csmaster_srs_* Spaced repetition flashcard data Persistent
csmaster_bookmarks Your saved bookmarks Persistent
csmaster_speedrun_pb_* Game high scores Persistent

Preference Storage

These remember your preferences:

Storage Key Purpose Duration
csmaster_onboarding_complete Remember if you've completed setup Persistent
csmaster_user_preferences Your exam date and topic preferences Persistent
csmaster_cookie_consent Your cookie consent choice 1 year

3. Third-Party Storage

We do not use third-party cookies or tracking technologies. External resources we use (Google Fonts, Font Awesome, cdnjs) are loaded directly without setting cookies.

4. Managing Browser Storage

You can manage browser storage in several ways:

Clear All Site Data

In most browsers:

  • Open Developer Tools (F12 or right-click → Inspect)
  • Go to Application tab
  • Click "Clear site data" in the Storage section

Note: This will log you out and delete all your progress.

Clear Specific Data

In your browser settings, you can typically find options to clear:

  • Cookies and site data
  • Cached images and files
  • Local storage data

In-App Options

Within CS Master Pro, you can:

  • Delete your account (removes all your data)
  • Reset progress from the settings menu

5. Your Consent

By using CS Master Pro, you consent to our use of essential browser storage. We will ask for your consent before using any non-essential storage technologies.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser data, though this will affect your ability to use the application.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
