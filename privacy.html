Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CS Master Pro ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our educational web application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and encrypted password.
  • Learning Progress: We store your quiz scores, achievements, XP points, and study progress to personalize your learning experience.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, including pages visited, features used, and time spent studying.
  • Device Information: Basic device information for compatibility and performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our educational services
  • Track your learning progress and provide personalized recommendations
  • Save your achievements, scores, and study streaks
  • Improve our application and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about your account or updates

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • We do not transmit your personal data to external servers
  • Clearing your browser data will remove your stored progress
  • Your data is not accessible to other users of the same device unless they use your browser profile

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use browser storage technologies (localStorage) to:

  • Remember your login session
  • Store your preferences (sound settings, theme)
  • Save your learning progress offline

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that collect personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

Our application uses the following third-party services for functionality:

  • Google Fonts: For typography (font files only, no tracking)
  • Font Awesome: For icons (CDN delivery only)
  • cdnjs/jsdelivr: For code libraries (no personal data shared)

7. Children's Privacy

CS Master Pro is designed for students, including those under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data (visible in your profile)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your progress data
  • Opt out of any non-essential data collection

9. Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Password hashing using SHA-256 encryption
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
  • Input validation and XSS protection
  • HTTPS encryption for all connections

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@csmasterpro.com
